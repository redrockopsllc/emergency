🧰 What to do, by zone

🟡 GET SET zone (evacuation warning)

  • Pack a go-bag now: meds, documents, chargers, cash, glasses, 3 days of clothes
  • The 6 P's: People & pets · Prescriptions · Papers · Personal needs · Priceless items · Phone
  • Fuel the car; park it nose-out, ready to leave
  • Know two routes out of your neighborhood
  • Photograph/video your home and belongings for insurance
  • Load pets' carriers, leashes, food now — not at the last minute
  • Register for Washoe alerts and turn on Watch Duty notifications

🔴 GO NOW zone (evacuation order)

  • Leave immediately — do not wait to see flames or pack more
  • Take your go-bag, pets, and every person in the household
  • Close windows and doors on your way out
  • Follow posted routes; never drive around barricades or into smoke
  • Text family + your manager: where you're headed and that you're out
  • Do not return until officials clear your zone on Perimeter

😮‍💨 Everyone else in the Reno area (smoke)

  • Watch the AQI panel above — over 150, limit time outdoors
  • Wear an N95/KN95 outside (cloth masks don't stop smoke)
  • Keep windows shut; set HVAC to recirculate
  • No HEPA purifier? Build a box-fan filter (EPA guide)
  • Skip outdoor workouts; check on kids, elderly neighbors, and anyone with asthma/heart conditions
  • Feeling overwhelmed? Call or text 988 — free, 24/7
